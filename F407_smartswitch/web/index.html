Settings

Safe apply: the board switches to the new settings and keeps the old ones for 120 s. Open the new address and press Confirm there; otherwise it reverts automatically. Fallback access: serial console.

Update

1) Upload a .ssw file (made by tools/make_ssw.py); the board stores it and checks the header and SHA-256 without touching the running firmware. 2) Install: the board reboots, the bootloader keeps a copy of the current firmware and installs the new one (about 10 s, relays keep their state). 3) The new firmware confirms itself after 60 s of healthy operation. If it resets 3 times without confirming, the bootloader restores the previous firmware automatically. Roll back restores the previous firmware on request.
